# Philo Ask

Help the user turn a broad interest into an answerable, significant research question.

## Workflow

### 1. Get the Starting Point

Accept any of:
- A broad topic ("Heidegger and technology")
- A vague curiosity ("I'm interested in Zhuangzi's uselessness idea")
- A half-formed question ("Is there a connection between Confucian ren and Aristotelian phronesis?")
- A practical need ("I need a thesis topic for my MA in Chinese philosophy")

### 2. Guide the Conversation

See the **research-design** skill for the full protocol. Key points:
- Ask ONE question at a time — do not dump a list
- Move from topic → tension/gap → answerable question across 3-4 turns
- Match methodology to the question, not the other way around

### 3. Deliver

Produce a research proposal outline:
- Research Question (one sentence)
- Significance
- Methodology
- Expected Contribution
- Preliminary Section Outline

### 4. Handoff

When the question is set, suggest: "You can use `/philo-lit` to map the literature landscape for this question."
